A transcript is the text of one radio transmission. You can search transcripts by words, callsign, time, airport, channel or speaker, and download the audio of a single transmission.

| | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Transcript | 3 credits ($0.03), or $30 per 1,000 |
| Audio clip | Banded by monthly volume, from 0.3 credits ($0.003). See [audio rates](/concepts/pricing#audio-rates). |

## List transcripts

<div className="not-prose">`GET /transcripts`</div>

<Note>Parameter names are placeholders until the v1 OpenAPI spec is final.</Note>

<ParamField query="q" type="string">
  Words to search for in the transcript text.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="callsign" type="string">
  Aircraft callsign, for example `UAL123`.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="airport" type="string">
  Airport ICAO code, for example `KSFO`.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="channel" type="string">
  Radio channel or frequency.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="speaker" type="string">
  Who was speaking, for example a controller or a pilot.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="start" type="string">
  Start of the time window, in UTC.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="end" type="string">
  End of the time window, in UTC.
</ParamField>

```bash
curl "$ER_API_BASE/transcripts?airport=KSFO&start=2026-10-24T18:00:00Z&end=2026-10-24T19:00:00Z" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ER_API_KEY"
```

## Audio

<div className="not-prose">`GET /transcripts/{id}/audio`</div>

Returns the audio clip for one transmission.

- Audio needs **paid credits**.
- Each clip is one transmission. There's no bulk or continuous audio.
- Downloading the same clip again is free for 30 days.
- An account can download at most **1,000 clips** until it is at least 36 hours old **and** has made $1,000 in successful payments. Prepaid credits count even if unused; free credits don't. This cap doesn't reset.

## Live transcripts

To have new transcripts sent to you, create a [subscription](/api-reference/subscriptions) with the `transcripts` dataset.
