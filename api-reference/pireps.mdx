Pilot reports (PIREPs) of weather and runway conditions, with the time, location and height when known, and their sources.

| | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Pilot report | 15 credits ($0.15), or $150 per 1,000 |

Rebroadcasts and corrections are free. Reading a report you bought in the last 30 days is free.

<Note>
  These reports come from radio and may not be official FAA pilot reports. Sources and missing locations are shown.
</Note>

## Query PIREPs

<div className="not-prose">`POST /pireps/query`</div>

<Note>Field names are placeholders until the v1 OpenAPI spec is final.</Note>

<ParamField body="description" type="string">
  The conditions you want, in plain language, for example `moderate turbulence` or `braking action`.
</ParamField>
<ParamField body="filters" type="object">
  Narrow the results.
  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ParamField body="callsign" type="string">Aircraft callsign.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="area" type="object">The area to search.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="start" type="string">Start time, in UTC.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="end" type="string">End time, in UTC.</ParamField>
  </Expandable>
</ParamField>

## Live reports

To have new reports sent to you, create a [subscription](/api-reference/subscriptions) with the `pireps` dataset, using the same description and filters.

## What it costs

For 100 flights a day with 5 new reports per flight, PIREPs cost **$75 a day**, or **$27,375 a year**. Coverage varies.
