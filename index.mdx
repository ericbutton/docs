Enhanced Radar records US air traffic control radio from 200 receivers and transcribes it with our own model. The API turns those recordings into six products you can build on.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Transcripts & Audio" icon="message-square-text" href="/api-reference/transcripts">

  </Card>

  <Card title="Events" icon="bell" href="/api-reference/events">

  <Card title="Flight Status" icon="plane" href="/api-reference/flight-status">

## How it works

- **Pull or push.** Pull: your app requests data. Push: we send new data to your webhook as it arrives. See [Pull and push](/concepts/pull-and-push).
- **Choose what to follow.** Filter by callsign and time, an airport, or an area.
- **Pay per item.** Prices are in credits, and 1 credit = \$0.01. There's no minimum volume, card or monthly plan. See [Pricing and credits](/concepts/pricing).
- **Check the source.** Each fact shows when it was reported, where it came from and what's missing. See [Sources and coverage](/concepts/sources-and-coverage).
