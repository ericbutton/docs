title: "Create answer"
description: "Answers a question about aviation operations from radio transcripts, ADS-B, FAA SWIM records, weather, and the web, with the evidence behind it."
api: "POST /api/v0/answers"
---

Answers a question about aviation operations by researching radio transcripts from channels available to the API, ADS-B aircraft positions, FAA SWIM records (ground stops, flow programs, EDCTs, holds, diversions, airport configuration, surface movements), weather, and the public web. Returns the answer and the records it rests on.

Nothing is stored between requests. To ask a follow-up, send the earlier questions and answers again in `messages`, with the new question last. Earlier answers are used only to understand what the new question refers to; every answer is researched again from the records.

<Note>
  An answer usually takes from several seconds to a few minutes. Keep the connection open until the response arrives.
</Note>

## Body

JSON, at most 256 KiB.

<ParamField body="messages" type="object[]" required>
  The conversation, oldest first, 1 to 40 messages. Each is `{"role": "user" | "assistant", "content": "..."}`. The last message must have role `user`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="format" default="text" type="string">
  `text`, `markdown`, or `json`. `markdown` quotes transmissions as links of the form `[words](audio:<audio_sample_id>)`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="json_schema" type="object">
  Required with `format` `json`; not accepted with other formats. A JSON Schema object describing the answer, e.g. `{"type": "object", "properties": {"diverted": {"type": "boolean"}}}`. The answer is written to follow it but is not validated against it.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="record_limit" default="5000" type="integer">
  Most records to review, 300 to 50000. Research stops when it is reached, and the answer says what could not be checked. Lowered to what the organization's available balance covers.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="evidence_limit" default="25" type="integer">
  Most evidence records to return, 0 to 50.
</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="format" type="string">
  The format of the answer.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="answer_text" type="string">
  The answer, for formats `text` and `markdown`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="answer_json" type="any">
  The answer, for format `json`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="evidence" type="object">
  The records the answer rests on, at most `evidence_limit` in total, most important first within each list. Every record was retrieved while answering.

  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ResponseField name="transcripts" type="array">
      Transmissions, in the shape of [Get transcript](/api-reference/transcripts/get).
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="swim_records" type="array">
      FAA SWIM records: `source_tool` names the kind of record (`surface_timeline`, `ground_stops`, `airport_config`, `edcts`, `holds`, or `diversions`), `source_record_id` identifies it, and `record` holds its fields.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="adsb_positions" type="array">
      ADS-B aircraft positions: `icao_hex`, `callsign`, `registration`, `seen_at`, `latitude`, `longitude`, `altitude_ft_baro` (feet, or `"ground"`), `ground_speed_kts`, `track_degrees`, and `squawk`.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="web_pages" type="array">
      Web pages that were read: `url`, `title`, and `published_at` when the page states it.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="records_reviewed" type="object">
  Transmissions, ADS-B positions, and SWIM records reviewed while answering, and their `total`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="record_limit" type="integer">
  The record limit used, after lowering it to what the organization's available balance covers.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="is_record_limit_reached" type="boolean">
  Whether research stopped at the record limit.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="records_charged" type="integer">
  Records charged: `records_reviewed.total`, at least 300 and at most `record_limit`.
</ResponseField>

<Warning>
  Answers are written by a language model from the records it reviewed and can contain mistakes. Check important details against the evidence.
</Warning>

## Billing

Each record reviewed costs \$0.01 USD, with a \$3.00 minimum per answer (300 records). For example, the 418 records charged in the response below cost \$4.18. Current rates and the billing-unit conversion are returned by `GET /api/v0/pricing`.

The `X-Credits-Charged` and `X-Credit-Balance` response headers report the charge and remaining balance as integer billing units. Convert either value to USD with `header_value * usd_cents_per_credit / 100`, using `usd_cents_per_credit` from the pricing response. At the current conversion of \$0.01 per unit, a charge header of \`418\` means \$4.18, and a balance header of `100000` means \$1,000.00. The header names are part of the API contract; their raw values are not dollar amounts.

## Errors

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `400` | The body is not valid JSON, or a field is missing or invalid. `error` says which. |
| `402` | The organization's available balance does not cover the \$3.00 minimum (300 records). |
| `502` | The answer could not be produced. Nothing is charged. Retry later. |

<RequestExample>

```bash cURL
curl -X POST -H "Authorization: Bearer $ER_API_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  "https://api.enhancedradar.com/api/v0/answers" \
  -d '{"messages": [{"role": "user", "content": "Did United 254 divert last night?"}], "format": "json", "json_schema": {"type": "object", "properties": {"diverted": {"type": "boolean"}, "diversion_airport": {"type": "string"}}}}'
```
