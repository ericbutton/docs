The API is priced in credits. **1 credit = $0.01.** You pay per item, with no minimum volume.

## Credits

- Every account starts with **100,000 free credits ($1,000)**.
- Buy more in three sizes:

| Credits | Price |
| --- | --- |
| 1,000 | $10 |
| 10,000 | $100 |
| 100,000 | $1,000 |

- No card or monthly plan is required. Paid credits never expire.
- Fractional credits are counted exactly.
- Records you've bought are free to read again for 30 days.
- Audio needs paid credits. Free credits can't be used for audio.

### What's free

- Reading a record you bought in the last 30 days
- Retries and corrections
- Rewording of a Flight Status or Airport Status update (you pay only for the first status and each real change)
- Rebroadcasts of a PIREP
- Charging the same event twice: each event is charged once

### Audio rates

Audio is one radio transmission per clip. Each rate applies only to the clips in that band, and counts reset monthly.

| Clips this month | Credits per clip | USD per clip |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 – 1,000 | 0.3 | $0.003 |
| 1,001 – 5,000 | 10 | $0.10 |
| 5,001 – 10,000 | 50 | $0.50 |
| 10,001 – 15,000 | 200 | $2.00 |
| 15,001 – 50,000 | 800 | $8.00 |
| 50,001 + | 3,138 | $31.38 |

For example, 1,000 clips a month costs $3, and 10,000 clips a month costs $2,903.

<Note>
  **Audio access cap.** An account can download at most 1,000 clips until it is at least 36 hours old **and** has made $1,000 in successful payments. Prepaid credits count even if unused; free credits don't. This cap doesn't reset.
</Note>

### Answers rates

| Records checked | Credits per record | USD per record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First 1,000 | 2 | $0.02 |
| Next 4,000 | 0.25 | $0.0025 |
| After 5,000 | 0.04 | $0.0004 |

So 1,000 records cost $20, 5,000 cost $30 and 50,000 cost $48. Each answer costs at least $3. See [Answers](/api-reference/answers) for limits.
