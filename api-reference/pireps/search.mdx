Searches pilot weather reports (PIREPs) heard on frequency near a point: a pilot reporting turbulence, icing, wind shear, cloud layers, or other conditions they encountered. A controller passing an earlier report on to other aircraft is not a new PIREP. Describe what you are looking for in plain words; results are ranked by how closely they match its meaning, not by exact words.

Each PIREP is returned as it was first reported, with its plain-language summary and the conditions encoded in the FAA PIREP format.

## Query parameters

<ParamField query="q" type="string" required>
  What you are looking for, e.g. `turbulence`, `icing on descent`, or `wind shear on final`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="since" type="string" required>
  Start of the window, inclusive. See [Times](/concepts/times).
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="until" type="string" required>
  End of the window, exclusive. Must be after `since` and at most 6 hours later.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="latitude" type="number" required>
  Latitude of the center of the search area, in decimal degrees.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="longitude" type="number" required>
  Longitude of the center of the search area, in decimal degrees.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="radius_nm" type="number" required>
  Radius of the search area in nautical miles, 1 to 150. A PIREP is in the area when the reporting aircraft was.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="limit" default="10" type="integer">
  Maximum PIREPs to return, 1 to 25.
</ParamField>

Results are the best matches in one page; there is no cursor. Weak matches are left out, so a search for conditions nobody reported returns no PIREPs. A PIREP is returned only when the reporting aircraft's position is known. A page can hold fewer than `limit` PIREPs even when more match. A PIREP can take a few minutes after it is heard to become searchable.

## Response

<ResponseField name="pireps" type="array">
  The PIREPs, best match first.

  <Expandable title="PIREP">
    <ResponseField name="event_id" type="integer">
      Id of the event the PIREP was reported in. Fetch it with [Get event](/api-reference/events/get).
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="atc_channel_id" type="integer">
      The channel the PIREP was heard on.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="facility_ids" type="string[]">
      Facilities that channel belongs to.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="happened_at" type="string">
      When the PIREP was made, in UTC, accurate to within 20 seconds.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="callsign" type="string">
      ICAO callsign of the reporting aircraft.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="aircraft_latitude" type="number">
      Latitude of the reporting aircraft from ADS-B when it reported, in decimal degrees.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="aircraft_longitude" type="number">
      Longitude of the reporting aircraft from ADS-B when it reported, in decimal degrees.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="summary" type="string">
      One-line plain-language summary of the conditions the pilot reported, with the pilot's words from the frequency.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="faa_pirep" type="string">
      The report in the FAA PIREP format of FAA Order JO 7110.10, starting with `UUA` for an urgent report or `UA` for a routine one. See [FAA PIREP format](#faa-pirep-format).
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="evidence_audio_sample_ids" type="string[]">
      Transmissions the PIREP was heard in. Fetch them with [Get transcript](/api-reference/transcripts/get) and [Get transcript audio](/api-reference/transcripts/audio).
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="search_score" type="number">
      Relevance of the match; higher is better. Compare scores only within one response.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## FAA PIREP format

`faa_pirep` holds these text elements, in this order, each only when known:

| Element | Source |
| --- | --- |
| `UUA` or `UA` | Urgent when the report includes a tornado, funnel cloud, or waterspout, severe or extreme turbulence, severe icing, hail, low-level wind shear, or volcanic ash. |
| `/TM` | `happened_at` as `HHMM` UTC. |
| `/FL` | The aircraft's barometric altitude from ADS-B in hundreds of feet, or `UNKN`. |
| `/TP` | Always `UNKN`; the aircraft type is not known. |
| `/SK`, `/WX`, `/TA`, `/WV`, `/TB`, `/IC`, `/RM` | Sky condition, flight visibility and weather, air temperature, wind, turbulence, icing, and remarks, as the pilot stated them. |

There is no `/OV` location element; use `aircraft_latitude` and `aircraft_longitude`.

<Warning>
  The condition elements are written by a language model from transcripts and can contain mistakes. Check important details against the transcripts.
</Warning>

## Billing

Each PIREP returned is charged as one `pirep`. A search that returns nothing costs nothing. See [Credits and billing](/concepts/billing).

## Errors

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `400` | A required parameter is missing, or a parameter is invalid. |
| `504` | The search took too long. Try again. |
