## Where the data comes from

Enhanced Radar records US air traffic control radio from **200 receivers**. Our own transcription model turns each radio transmission into text. Flight Status, Airport Status, Events, PIREPs and Answers are all built from those transcripts.

## Checking the source

Every fact we return tells you:

- **when** it was reported
- **where** it came from: the transcripts behind it
- **what's missing**: facts we couldn't find are listed, not guessed

Reading the sources behind a status or answer is included in its price. Downloading the full source records, or their audio, costs extra.

## Coverage limits

- **Partial flights.** We may capture only part of a flight, depending on which frequencies our receivers hear.
- **Coverage varies** by airport and area.
- **Duplicate recordings.** Several receivers may hear the same transmission. Duplicates may lower your bill.
- **PIREPs from radio** may not be official FAA pilot reports.
- **Route conditions** describe what pilots reported, not whether a navigation aid is working.
