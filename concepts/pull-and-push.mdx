Every product can be read in one of two ways:

- **Pull:** your app requests data. Use this for past data or occasional checks.
- **Push:** we send new data to your webhook as soon as it's ready. Use this for live updates.

## Choosing what to follow

Both pull and push take the same filters. Depending on the product, you can choose:

- a **callsign and time**
- an **airport**
- an **area**
- a **channel** or **speaker** (Transcripts)
- a plain-language **description** of what you want (Events and PIREPs)

## Push: subscriptions

To get pushed data, create a subscription with [`POST /subscriptions`](/api-reference/subscriptions). It names a dataset, your filters and your webhook URL.

| Dataset | Product |
| --- | --- |
| `transcripts` | [Transcripts](/api-reference/transcripts) |
| `events` | [Events](/api-reference/events) |
| `flight_status` | [Flight Status](/api-reference/flight-status) |
| `airport_status` | [Airport Status](/api-reference/airport-status) |
| `pireps` | [PIREPs](/api-reference/pireps) |

[Answers](/api-reference/answers) are scheduled differently: you save an answer with a schedule and a webhook.

## Webhook delivery

<Warning>
  **Draft.** Signing details and the exact payload envelope will be in the v1 reference.
</Warning>

- We send each update as a **signed HTTPS `POST`** to your webhook URL.
- Every update has a **unique ID** that stays the same across retries. Use it to ignore duplicates.
- Store the update before you return a `2xx` response.
- We **retry** temporary failures with backoff. Retries are free.
- If your endpoint was down, you can **recover missed updates for up to 24 hours**.

## Field conventions

- Times are in UTC, in ISO 8601 format, for example `2026-10-24T18:42:00Z`.
- Radio frequencies are in MHz, as a decimal string, for example `"120.500"`.
- Unknown values are `null`, and empty lists are `[]`. We never guess a missing value.
