The Enhanced Radar API gives you US air traffic control radio data: transcripts of ATC transmissions, the facilities and channels they come from, and what we derive from them, such as events, flight and airport statuses, and pilot weather reports.

Every route is under `https://api.enhancedradar.com`. All paths are under `/api/v0` except `/health`.

## What you can get

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Transcripts" icon="file-lines" href="/api-reference/transcripts/list">

  </Card>

  <Card title="Events" icon="triangle-exclamation" href="/api-reference/events/search">
    Notable things that happened on frequency, such as go-arounds, laser strikes, diversions, and gate returns.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Flight status" icon="plane" href="/api-reference/flights/status">
    The latest substantive ATC communication with one flight, in one sentence.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Airport status" icon="tower-control" href="/api-reference/airports/status">
    Runways, flow, traffic programs, weather, and what was heard on frequency at one airport.
  </Card>

  <Card title="PIREPs" icon="cloud-bolt" href="/api-reference/pireps/search">
    Pilot weather reports heard on frequency near a point, in plain language and FAA format.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Answers" icon="comments" href="/api-reference/answers/create">
    Ask a question and get an answer researched from transcripts, ADS-B, FAA SWIM, weather, and the web.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Pull or stream

- **Pull:** Transcript and event searches accept time windows of up to 48 hours; [PIREPs](/api-reference/pireps/search) are limited to 6 hours. See [Selecting data](/concepts/selecting-data).
- **Stream:** one WebSocket at `GET /api/v0/live` streams transcripts, events, flight statuses, airport statuses, and PIREPs as they happen. See [Live updates](/guides/live-updates).
