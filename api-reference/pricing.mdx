---
title: "Get pricing"
description: "GET /api/v0/pricing: returns the current US dollar price of each charged item, scheduled price changes, and account tiers with their limits."
api: "GET /api/v0/pricing"
---

Returns what each charged item of the Enhanced Radar API costs in US dollars, price changes scheduled for later, and the account tiers with their limits. This route is free and accepts the `api:onboarding` permission as well as `api:read`. See [Pricing](/pricing) for every price, and [Billing](/concepts/billing) for which routes charge which items.

## Response

<ResponseField name="current_prices" type="array">
  The price in effect now for each item.

  <Expandable title="price">
    <ResponseField name="priced_item" type="string">
      The item: `transcript`, `transcript_audio`, `event_query_page`, `event_pull`, `event_push`, `flight_status_update`, `airport_status_update`, `pirep`, or `answer_record`.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="unit_name" type="string">
      What one unit of the item is, e.g. `transcript` or `audio clip`.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="item_description" type="string">
      What the item is charged for.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="price_band_period" type="string">
      `month` when a unit's band depends on how many of the item the organization has been charged for in the calendar month (UTC), counting that unit. `none` when the price does not depend on earlier usage.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="effective_at" type="string">
      When this price took or takes effect.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="price_bands" type="array">
      The rates, lowest `from_quantity` first. A unit is charged the `usd_per_unit` of the band whose range contains its number.

      <Expandable title="band">
        <ResponseField name="from_quantity" type="integer">
          First unit number of the band.
        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="to_quantity" type="integer | null">
          Last unit number of the band. `null` for the last band.
        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="usd_per_unit" type="number">
          Price of each unit in the band, in US dollars with three decimal places, e.g. `0.030`.
        </ResponseField>
      </Expandable>
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="scheduled_prices" type="array">
  Prices that take effect later, at their `effective_at`, in the same shape as `current_prices`. Empty when none are scheduled.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="account_tiers" type="array">
  The account tiers. An organization is in the tier with the highest `minimum_amount_paid_usd_cents` whose minimums it meets.

  <Expandable title="tier">
    <ResponseField name="account_tier_name" type="string">
      `provisional`, `growth`, or `enterprise`.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="minimum_account_age_hours" type="integer">
      How old the organization's account must be, in hours.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="minimum_amount_paid_usd_cents" type="integer">
      How much the organization must have paid in total, in US cents.
    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="limits" type="array">
      The tier's limits. Empty when it has none. Each limit has `priced_item`, `limit_period` (`month` for a limit per calendar month in UTC, or `lifetime` for a limit over the life of the account), and `maximum_quantity`, the most units of the item the organization may be charged for in the period.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<RequestExample>

```bash cURL
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $ER_API_KEY" \
  "https://api.enhancedradar.com/api/v0/pricing"
```

</RequestExample>
