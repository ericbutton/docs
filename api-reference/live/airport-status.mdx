Streams one airport's status. Each update's `data` is a complete status with the same fields as [Get airport status](/api-reference/airports/status); replace the previous one with it. See [Connect](/api-reference/live/connect) for the message format.

## Parameters

<ParamField body="facility_id" type="string" required>
  An airport `facility_id` from [List facilities](/api-reference/facilities/list). TRACON and center facilities are refused.
</ParamField>

```json
{"action": "subscribe", "subscription_topic": "airport_status", "params": {"facility_id": "23b485da-06ec-4d7d-8dc3-aa45e7e809c9"}}
```

## Updates

The first update is the current status, sent right after `subscribed`. After that, a new status is sent:

- Each time an observation at the airport is created or updated.
- Each time `operations` and `weather` are recomputed: within 5 minutes when they change, and at least every 10 minutes otherwise.

Observations that age out of the 60-minute window do not cause an update; they are left out of the next one. Use `generated_at` to order updates and to ignore one older than a status you already have.

## Billing

Each status sent is charged as one `airport_status_update`, at \$1.50, including the first, which is charged when you subscribe. Subscribing again, including after a reconnect, charges a new first update. See [Pricing](/pricing) and [Billing](/concepts/billing).

## Refusals

The subscription is refused when `facility_id` is missing or not a uuid, is not a covered facility, or is not an airport; when the charge for the first update is refused; or when the airport status could not be loaded; retry that last case later.
